Your Safe Start in Germany.

Your arrival, setup, and first steps guided with clarity, care, and personal support.

GloBerry supports international students as they navigate arrival, registration, housing search, setup of essentials, and community integration. Our team guides you through every requirement with clarity and care, so you can focus on starting your new life in Berlin without not stressing about paperwork or what to do next.

About GloBerry

GloBerry was created by students who lived the journey themselves. We all faced the same early challenges, the same uncertainty, and the same search for reliable support. We learned through experience how important it is to have someone who understands what you are going through and can guide you step by step. That experience became the foundation of GloBerry.

Our purpose is to make your first weeks in Germany simple and well supported. We help you understand what needs to be done, assist with the essential tasks, and make sure you never feel lost in the process. Everything we offer is practical, straightforward, and built around what real students actually need when they first arrive.

Our ambition is to build a welcoming space where international students feel confident, informed, and at home from the moment they step into the country. We want GloBerry to grow into a trusted community where guidance is clear, support is reliable, and every student knows they have someone to turn to. Through our work, we aim to shape a smoother, more connected experience for anyone starting a new chapter in Germany.
